ABOUT THE DIVISION

BEYOND BIOLOGY

The Cybernetics Division develops technologies capable of integrating artificial structures with biological systems.

Our research focuses on advanced prosthetics, biomechanical interfaces, artificial limbs and adaptive systems designed to operate alongside the human body.

CURRENT RESEARCH

01

ADAPTIVE PROSTHETICS

STATUS: OPERATIONAL

Artificial limbs capable of adapting their movement patterns according to the user's neural signals.

Current prototypes demonstrate improved precision, balance and response time.

02

SYNTHETIC MUSCLE SYSTEMS

STATUS: DEVELOPMENT

Artificial muscle structures designed to reproduce the contraction and movement patterns of biological tissue.

Research is focused on improving strength, flexibility and biological compatibility.

03

BIOMECHANICAL FRAMEWORK

STATUS: RESTRICTED

Development of an internal artificial framework capable of supporting biological structures while maintaining natural movement.
