ABOUT THE DIVISION

THE NEXT FRONTIER

The Neural Systems Division focuses on developing technologies capable of interpreting, translating and responding to biological neural activity.

Our research combines neuroscience, artificial intelligence, cybernetics and biomedical engineering to create systems designed to operate alongside the human nervous system.

CURRENT RESEARCH

01

NEURAL-VR

STATUS: OPERATIONAL

Experimental virtual reality systems designed to study neural responses during controlled immersive environments.

Researchers investigate whether virtual motor simulations can assist the brain in rebuilding damaged neural pathways.

02

SYNAPTIC MOTION CHIP

STATUS: CLINICAL TRIAL

A neural interface designed to interpret electrical signals associated with voluntary movement.

The system translates neural activity into commands capable of controlling external assistive devices.
